# Product: CA Rohadas

CA Practice Management Software for Indian CA firms.

## Purpose
Manages clients, tasks/compliance deadlines, documents, billing/invoicing, payments/receipts, receivables, services, team, reports. Built for Indian chartered accountant practices.

## Users
Chartered accountants, articles/assistants, partners in CA firms.

## Mode
Operate - the visitor completes tasks. App UI, dashboards, editors, admin. Scanability, consistency, native expectations matter. Brand lives in precise details.

## Constraints
- Must preserve working APIs, authentication, database models and MCP tools
- Must not bypass MCP for business data operations
- Preserve tenant isolation
- Indian context (INR, GST, etc.)
- Must work with existing data

## Aesthetic Goals
- Serious, premium accounting product designed by experienced product-design team
- Distinctive visual identity, not typical AI SaaS
- Avoid purple gradients, excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism everywhere, repetitive three-column card grids, decorative AI-style glow effects
- Intentional motion, meaningful transitions, high-quality micro-interactions
- Respect accessibility, reduced-motion, mobile responsiveness
